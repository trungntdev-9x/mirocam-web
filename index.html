Engine & Architecture

The Science Behind The Shot

Unlike ordinary camera apps that merely slap an Instagram filter over compressed JPEGs, Mirocam reconstructs color chemistry right from the sensor's raw mosaic.

Native NDK RAW Pipeline with LibRaw

On RAW-capable devices, Mirocam intercepts uncompressed Bayer sensor data via Camera2 API. Our custom C++ NDK engine performs demosaicing, tone-mapping curve interpolation, and precision 3D LUT transformations at 33³ or 64³ resolution before encoding to ultra-clean 10-bit JPEG or HEIC.

Privacy Policy & Transparency

Last updated: September 2026

1. Zero Data Collection: Mirocam was built from the ground up to respect user sovereignty. The application does not collect, track, transmit, or store any personal information, telemetry, usage metrics, or location data.

2. Permissions:
• CAMERA: Used strictly to capture live sensor streams and photos within the app viewfinder.
• READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used solely to save your captured photos and load custom user LUT files from your storage.

3. No Network Traffic: Mirocam does not declare Internet permissions in its AndroidManifest.xml. It operates entirely offline.
