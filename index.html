Custom Software RAW ISP (C++17 & OpenMP)

100% custom software ISP built from scratch in C++17 NDK with OpenMP multi-threading. Directly processes uncompressed sensor Bayer data (RAW_SENSOR / RAW10 / RAW12) through black level subtraction, hot-pixel suppression, wavelet BayesShrink chroma denoise, fused demosaicing, and AMD CAS sharpening before encoding.

Custom C++17 NDKOpenMP Multi-threadingBayer DemosaicAMD CAS Sharpening

ZSL Ring Buffer & Robust Burst Stack

Zero Shutter Lag with circular Bayer frame buffering. Upon shutter release, Mirocam merges bursts using HDR+-style alignment with robust motion and clipped-sample rejection, yielding pristine shadow SNR with no motion blur, computed asynchronously in background.

Zero Shutter LagHDR+ Burst MergeMotion RejectionBackground Service

D-Range Highlight Recovery Engine

Advanced D-Range processing (OFF, MED, HIGH) with photometric exposure anchoring and adaptive headroom control. Protects blown skies and harsh neon speculars while gracefully rolling off shoulder highlights without crushing shadow blacks.
