SECTION 01

Introduction & On-Device Camera Promise

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for Mirocam ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), package ID com.mirocam.app. Mirocam is an Android mobile camera and darkroom application engineered to capture RAW sensor streams, apply 3D LUT film simulations, and render halation, mist, and film grain directly on your phone.

Zero Network Activity During Camera Startup & Capture

Opening Mirocam, previewing presets, capturing RAW/JPEG photos or film video, and editing in the in-app gallery make zero network calls. Network connectivity is used strictly when Google Play Billing checks/purchases a Miro Pro license, or when you explicitly open or interact with the optional MiroCam Lookbook community feature.

SECTION 02

Information We Never Collect

To provide clear transparency, Mirocam never collects, tracks, or monetizes the following data:

  • No Background or Automatic Photo Uploads: Private photos, videos, or RAW DNG captures saved on your device are never backed up or transmitted automatically.
  • No Usage Telemetry or Analytics: We do not collect behavioral analytics, crash profiling beacons, lens/preset usage counters, or session duration logs.
  • No Advertising Identifiers: We do not read or share Google Advertising ID (AAID), IMEI, MAC addresses, or hardware serial numbers.
  • No Biometric or Facial Recognition: Our image pipeline applies colorimetric and geometric transforms only; it never runs facial recognition, biometric identification, or demographic profiling.
  • No Precise Location Harvesting: Mirocam does not declare Android GPS/location permissions, and Lookbook automatically strips any embedded GPS coordinates from sample photos before upload.
SECTION 03

Optional MiroCam Lookbook (Community, Sign-In & EXIF Stripping)

MiroCam Lookbook (lookbook.lagbros.com and media.lagbros.com) is a completely optional community directory where photographers browse film recipes, test them live on their viewfinder, and share their own presets or custom LUTs.

  • Anonymous Browsing & Install Counts: No account or sign-in is required to open Lookbook, test-drive presets live in the camera, or save presets/LUTs to your device. When you save or test a public recipe, Lookbook prevents duplicate counting using a one-way daily salted cryptographic hash (HMAC-SHA256 with a rotating daily server pepper) and does not store your raw IP address long-term.
  • Optional Google Sign-In: You only sign in (via Android Credential Manager with your Google account) if you explicitly choose to Like, Bookmark, Report / Block, or Publish a recipe. When you sign in, Lookbook stores your Google account subject identifier (sub), display name, auto-generated public handle, profile avatar URL (if any), and email address solely for authentication, author attribution, abuse prevention, and account deletion. Local access/refresh tokens on your phone are encrypted via Android Keystore (AES-256-GCM) in a private store excluded from Android cloud backups.
  • User-Published Recipes & Automatic EXIF / GPS Stripping: When you publish a recipe to Lookbook, Mirocam uploads only the recipe parameters, title/description/tags, an optional validated custom LUT (.look or .cube), and the specific reference before/after photos you select. Before any image leaves your phone, Mirocam decodes it locally, resizes it to at most 1440px, re-encodes to clean WebP, and verifies at the RIFF container level that all EXIF, GPS location, XMP, and device metadata chunks have been completely stripped. The backend re-verifies every uploaded WebP header before publication; incomplete staged uploads under quarantine/ expire automatically within 24 hours.
  • User Moderation & Safety Controls: Signed-in users can report violating content or block another creator directly from any Lookbook item page. Blocked creators and their items are filtered immediately from your feed.
SECTION 04

Android Permissions & Why We Need Them

Mirocam requests only the minimal hardware and system permissions declared in AndroidManifest.xml:

android.permission.CAMERARequired

Purpose: Essential for accessing your phone's physical image sensors via Android Camera2 to render the live viewfinder, manual exposure controls, autofocus, and photo/video capture strictly on-device.

android.permission.RECORD_AUDIOOptional

Purpose: Requested only if you record video with sound. If denied, Mirocam records silent video normally.

INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEOptional / Network

Purpose: Used exclusively for Google Play Billing / Play In-App Updates and optional user-initiated MiroCam Lookbook requests (browsing recipes, downloading custom LUTs, optional Google Sign-In, and publishing recipes). Zero network traffic occurs during camera startup or capture.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE & POST_NOTIFICATIONSSystem

Purpose: Keeps background RAW burst development and optional user-triggered Lookbook uploads reliable without OS interruption.

READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / STORAGERequired

Purpose: Used via Android Scoped Storage / MediaStore API to save rendered JPEG, MP4 video, or RAW DNG outputs to DCIM/Mirocam, display your in-app gallery, select optional reference photos to publish, and import custom .cube LUTs.

SECTION 05

RAW Sensor & Camera Data Pipeline

Mirocam connects directly to your phone's camera hardware through the native Android NDK and Camera2 API:

  • Sensor Ingestion: Uncompressed Bayer RAW sensor streams (RAW_SENSOR / RAW10 / RAW12) are buffered in local memory via a circular ZSL ring buffer.
  • Software ISP Processing: Demosaicing, BayesShrink chroma noise reduction, D-Range highlight recovery, and CAS sharpening run natively on-device via our custom C++17 OpenMP pipeline (libfilmsync_raw).
  • Volatile Memory: Intermediate frame buffers in RAM are discarded once frames finish rendering; no background frame capture or remote streaming occurs.
SECTION 06

Local Storage & Photo Ownership

Every photograph, video, and local preset created inside Mirocam remains yours:

  • Storage Directory: Captured photos and videos are saved to your device's standard gallery folders (DCIM/Mirocam / Movies/Mirocam).
  • User Deletion: You can view, share, or delete any local photo anytime from your gallery or file manager. Uninstalling Mirocam does not delete photos already saved to your gallery.
  • Custom LUTs & Presets: Imported .cube / .look files and local custom presets stay strictly in private on-device storage unless you explicitly choose to publish one to Lookbook.
SECTION 07

Third-Party Services, Infrastructure & SDKs

Mirocam takes an uncompromising stand against hidden data harvesting:

Zero Advertising or Analytics SDKs

• NO Google Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics
• NO Facebook / Meta SDK
• NO Advertising networks (Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin)
• NO Behavioral attribution trackers (Adjust, AppsFlyer, Branch)

The only external services Mirocam interacts with are:

  • Google Play Services: Official Google Play Billing Library (for Miro Pro purchases/trials), Play In-App Updates / Review, Credential Manager Google Sign-In (only if you sign in to Lookbook), and on-device QR recipe scanning (play-services-code-scanner).
  • Cloudflare Workers, D1 & R2 (Lookbook Infrastructure): Powers lookbook.lagbros.com and media.lagbros.com over HTTPS/TLS exclusively for optional Lookbook browsing, authentication, recipe sharing, moderation, and verified media hosting.
SECTION 08

Account Deletion, Data Retention & Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA / Google Play)

Under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and Google Play User Data policies, you have full control to access, review, and permanently delete any optional Lookbook account data:

  • Delete Inside the App: Open MiroCam → Lookbook → Account → Delete Lookbook Account (with recent Google re-authentication within 5 minutes for security).
  • Delete via Web (No App Required): Visit https://lookbook.lagbros.com/account/delete, sign in with the same Google account, and confirm permanent deletion.
  • What Deletion Removes Immediately: Your Lookbook user record, profile handle/avatar, active sessions/refresh tokens, likes, bookmarks, blocks, published recipes, custom LUT binaries, and uploaded sample photos stored in Cloudflare D1 and R2 are permanently deleted. Anonymous moderation/audit records retain zero personal identifiers.
  • Automatic Staging Cleanup: Incomplete or aborted media uploads in the private R2 quarantine/ prefix are blocked from public access and automatically expired within 24 hours.
  • Zero Sale of Data: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties.
SECTION 09

Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Mirocam is a general photography utility not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). Optional Lookbook accounts are not intended for users under 13. Because offline camera use collects zero personal data and optional Lookbook accounts require explicit Google authentication, if you believe a child under the applicable age threshold has created a Lookbook account, please contact trungntdev@gmail.com or use lookbook.lagbros.com/account/delete for immediate removal.
